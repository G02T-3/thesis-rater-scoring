How to score

  • Tian will tell you which questions apply to the run. Leave the others unselected.
  • For each question, tap 0, 1 or 2. The wording under each score is the official anchor.
  • You may add a short answer (for example a location or direction). Tian times each answer from the start of the clip and can enter the seconds.
  • Scores are saved on this phone after every tap, even if the browser closes.
  • At the end of the day, export the file and send it to Tian. Do not use the same phone as the other rater.
